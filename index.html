Contractor Dependency Model

VTB Bank · Workforce analytics

Top management needed to see which information systems depend on contractor staff. The request kept coming back as a one-off and took 8 hours by hand each time.

  • Automated model for about 50,000 IT employees across 18 production roles, split by category (staff, contractor, bank employee) in absolute and percentage terms.
  • The analysis became the basis for a process to control workforce shares and move key IS roles in-house.
